# Music

The chiptunes in this folder are by **Stennish**, from the playlist
[chiptunes](https://soundcloud.com/autistgamer1337/sets/chiptunes), and are licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Changes for NfoGlow: each track was shortened to its music (0:00–0:48, the silence after it removed) and
converted from WAV to Ogg Vorbis. NfoGlow plays the intro (0:00–0:08) once and then repeats the loop
(0:08–0:48), as the artist describes it.
